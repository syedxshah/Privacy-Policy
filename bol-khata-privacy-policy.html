Privacy Policy for Bol Khata

Last updated: October 03, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bol Khata ("the App"), a personal ledger app that helps you record and manage your daily expenses and accounts, handles your information. The App is developed and maintained by Syed Ansar Ali ("we," "us," or "our").

Summary

  • Bol Khata is a 100% local-only app.
  • We do not collect any personal information.
  • All your data, including your ledger entries and your authentication details (such as a PIN, password, or biometric lock), is stored offline in your device's own memory.
  • Nothing is sent to us or to any server. We cannot see, access, or recover your data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Bol Khata does not collect, transmit, or share any of the following:

  • Your name, phone number, email address, or address
  • Ledger entries, amounts, notes, or contact details you enter into the App
  • Login credentials, PINs, or passwords
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics
  • Crash reports or diagnostics

The App has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no cloud sync.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

Everything you enter into Bol Khata is saved only in the App's private storage on your device. This includes:

  • Ledger data: entries, balances, and any notes you add.
  • Authentication: any app lock or credentials you set are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded anywhere. If you use your device's biometrics (fingerprint or face unlock), that verification is handled entirely by your device's operating system. The App never receives or stores your biometric data.

Because nothing leaves your device, we cannot reset your password or recover your data if you forget your credentials or lose your device. Please keep your own backups if your records are important.

3. No Sharing, No Selling, No Third Parties

Since we collect no data, we have nothing to share, sell, rent, or disclose. The App does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking tools from third parties, and it does not require an internet connection to work.

4. Permissions

The App only requests device permissions that are needed for a feature you choose to use, and any such access stays on your device. If you export, share, or back up your data yourself (for example, as a file or a message), that happens at your direction and outside our control. Please be mindful of where you send it.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove it at any time by:

  • deleting individual entries within the App,
  • clearing the App's storage in your device settings, or
  • uninstalling the App, which permanently removes all locally stored data (unless you have made your own backups).

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

6. Children's Privacy

Bol Khata does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since no data is collected or transmitted, the App does not process children's personal information.

7. Security

Your data is protected by the security features of your device and the App's local storage. As the data never leaves your device, it is not exposed to online transmission or server breaches. You are responsible for keeping your device secure, for example with a screen lock and up-to-date software.

8. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or hold any personal information about you, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, port, or erase. You keep full control of everything in the App on your own device.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above. If a future version of the App ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that change takes effect.
